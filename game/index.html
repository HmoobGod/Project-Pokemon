Arena Slice

Survive the waves. Your hero attacks the nearest enemy automatically.

3 zones of 10 minutes, each on a new map. Level up, then wake the zone's Guardian at its altar when you're ready (stand on it and tap the action button / press E). Beat it to open the portal to the next zone. After zone 3 the portal leads to the Shrine: defeating its Legendary wins the run, then leave through the exit portal. When a zone's clock runs out the Final Swarm comes — you can keep fighting.

Move: WASD / arrow keys, or drag anywhere on a touch screen. Level-up: tap a card or press 1 / 2 / 3. Pause: ⏸ or Esc.
